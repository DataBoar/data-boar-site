How to evidence minors’ data in folders and spreadsheets — without coding

Direct answer: point folders and exports (enrollment, LMS, HR, clinic) at a local and offline scan that looks for age/date-of-birth columns and values and flags signals of possible minors’ data with high priority in the report (Excel + JSON). This supports the care called for by LGPD Art. 14 (and the GDPR Art. 8 parallel) as inventory and triage evidence — not as legal age verification, validated parental consent or a legal opinion.

Honest framing. Findings such as “possible minor” are signals for the DPO, school manager, HR or clinic to prioritize human review. The organization decides policy, legal basis and communication to the ANPD. DataBoar = a map and proof that you looked — no certification theater.

The risk that the “we have school records” inventory tends to miss — age/DOB in the same spreadsheet as the guardian’s CPF or health-like data (triage cross-reference). Many prepare for “do minors exist?”, not for where the signal concentrates alongside an identifier. Threshold and patterns are configurable (YAML); consulting/commercial support can tune the target — never legal age verification.

Why this question matters

Schools, edtechs, clinics, HR and accounting firms usually have school records, class exports, guardian lists or age spreadsheets scattered across folders and e-mails. The search “how to find minors’ data / Art. 14 without a DBA” almost always gets an answer about a developer library, an expensive catalog or a cloud suite. The missing path: no code, no sending the file to the cloud, with a readable report.

What the tool actually detects

  • Column names that suggest date of birth or age (Portuguese and English; e.g. data de nascimento, DDN, NASC, idade, DOB, age).
  • Sampled values — numeric age or dates that, read as a birth date, fall below the configurable threshold (default 18) → flags DOB_POSSIBLE_MINOR with maximum sensitivity in the report.
  • Cross-reference (when enabled): if the same file/path also has an identifier or health-like data, the report raises triage confidence (“high cross-ref”) — still as a signal, not a verdict.
  • Output: Excel spreadsheet for humans + JSON for machines; dedicated recommendation in the report.

Public technical documentation of the product: MINOR_DETECTION.pt_BR.md and the use case EdTech / LMS / minors.

How to do it (no DBA, no API)

  1. Light install — on Windows, the native path without Docker; validate with data-boar --demo (synthetic data). Guide: windows.html.
  2. Point at the folder — only the directory of test exports/enrollments (synthetic sample or controlled copy). Do not scan the whole disk.
  3. Generate the report — open the Excel, filter minor signals, check the file path. A due-diligence trail for the internal playbook; it does not replace the playbook.

What this does not do

  • It does not validate a legal guardian’s consent.
  • It does not issue an Art. 14 compliance seal or a legal opinion.
  • It does not moderate children’s chat or perform pedagogical assessment.
  • It does not make up example PII from real data — always use a synthetic sample at first contact (--demo).

Quick questions

How do I find minors’ data in my folders without coding?

By pointing folders/exports at the local discovery and reading the report. The engine looks for age/DOB signals; you review. Details in the FAQ.

What is differential Art. 14 treatment in inventory practice?

Prioritizing and highlighting in the report what may be a minor’s data, for human triage — aligned with the heightened care of the law, without deciding compliance.

Does the tool generate the report on its own?

Yes, after you point at the target. Legal interpretation and measures (access, retention, contracts) remain with the DPO/legal/organization.
