How our solution works

A simple, powerful, local-first process: processing and findings stay in your environment, and only metadata leaves — data content is never sent to the cloud.

1. Connect

Integrate your data sources in minutes — databases, files, S3-compatible object storage (Amazon S3, MinIO, Cloudflare R2, SeaweedFS), cloud, enterprise systems, and more.

2. Discover

DataBoar scans the environment and helps identify personal and sensitive data using deterministic techniques and smart sampling.

3. Classify

Classify information by sensitivity and context, visualize risk, and understand the business impact.

4. Generate Evidence

Produce executive summaries, technical reports, and structured inputs for records of processing activities, DPIA and RIPD — subject to review and validation by the controller, DPO, or compliance owner.

What sets DataBoar apart

Technology and methodology built for trust and real business impact.

Deterministic & Verifiable

Deterministic, verifiable AI (specialized ML/DL + a Clojure sidecar), no LLM/generative — reproducible and auditable. Sister tools in development: what they do today and what's next →

Governance with evidence

Honest scope: discovery → classification → evidence → remediation via plugin — auditable trails for your privacy program.

Light and portable

Designed to run from min-spec to datacenter, including air-gapped, with no heavy infrastructure. Install via Linux packages, PyPI (Linux, Windows, and macOS, with pip or pipx), or Docker; MSI installer in progress.

Built for teams

Intuitive dashboards and reports that make decisions easier.

Use cases

DataBoar adapts to different needs and sectors.

LGPD & Privacy

Supports LGPD, GDPR, and other frameworks with evidence — compliance remains the responsibility of the DPO and the organization.

Audit & Compliance

Speed up internal and external audits with structured, traceable reports.

Data Inventory

Keep an up-to-date inventory of personal and sensitive data across the organization.

Risk Management

Identify, assess, and prioritize risk to reduce exposure and protect the business.

Assess data assets and risk in mergers, acquisitions, and partnerships.
