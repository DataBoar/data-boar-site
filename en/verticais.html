LGPD data discovery by vertical

Straight answer: the tool is the same — a local scan, Excel report, no sending the client’s file to a vendor cloud. What changes is the sector pain: client folder, matter file, condo unit. Always inventory and triage, never legal advice or a compliance seal.

Beyond PII: DataBoar is not limited to personal data / LGPD. Through YAML configuration, the same engine can map other discoverable patterns useful for inventories and evidence aligned with other frameworks — flexible by design. To fit each organization’s reality, there is consulting and, under a commercial contract, support to tune the configuration. Still technical discovery, not legal advice.

Guides on this site

Each vertical includes at least one sector-typical case — a pain or risk that a generic “PII inventory” often misses.

  • Accounting — payroll/tax ZIPs for several clients on the same share (CNPJ/bank + CPF), not only “where’s the CPF.”
  • Law firms — signals of sensitive categories (e.g. criminal-nature material) and parties in PSTs from closed matters — without classifying privilege.
  • Condo / real estate — leftover former-tenant + guarantor + income folders after a unit turnover.
  • Comparison by category — local scanner × self-declaration × DPO-as-a-service × official register: four different questions, none replaces the others.
  • Minors / LGPD Art. 14 — age/DOB crossed with an identifier in the same export — triage signal, never age verification.
